Free business listing for Jackson County, Florida

The County Compass is a local directory for Jackson County, Florida — built for residents looking for services and for business owners who want to be found without paying for complicated ads first.

During our public beta, standard business listings are free to submit. No credit card is required to apply. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live.

Who should list

  • Handymen, plumbers, electricians, and home services
  • Lawn care, landscaping, and tractor work
  • Restaurants, shops, and local retail
  • Auto repair, towing, and mobile services
  • Professional services serving Marianna and nearby towns

What you get

  • A public listing on The County Compass business directory
  • Category placement so locals can find your type of service
  • Room for your contact details, description, and image
  • A simple way for neighbors to discover you outside random social posts

Towns we serve

Jackson County including Marianna, Graceville, Sneads, Malone, Cottondale, Greenwood, Grand Ridge, Alford, and surrounding areas.
