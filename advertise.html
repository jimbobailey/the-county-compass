Advertise With The County Compass

The County Compass helps connect local businesses, events, promotions, and sponsored advertising with the community.

This page is for requesting information and asking questions about our available advertising and listing options. Submitting this form does not automatically publish your business or create a paid order.

After submitting your request, we will contact you directly to discuss pricing, availability, placement options, and additional details.

Business Listing

Add your business to the local business directory so people can find your name, category, contact information, location, and details.

Best for businesses that want to be listed on the Businesses page.

Featured Placement

Upgrade your business listing to be featured near the top of selected pages for extra visibility.

Best for businesses that want more attention across the site.

Coupon Promotion

Share a sale, discount, special offer, or limited-time deal on the Coupons page.

Best for restaurants, shops, services, and local deals.

Event Promotion

Promote a local event such as a fundraiser, church event, school event, concert, festival, sale, or community gathering.

Best for upcoming events that need local attention.

Sponsored Ad

Place a sponsored ad that can appear across site pages in sponsored advertising areas.

Best for businesses that want their ad image shown around the site.
